Profile

I build robots that leave the simulator, from quadrupeds navigating a campus to quadrotors that keep flying when a rotor fails.

I’m a master’s student in Mechatronics and Cyber-Physical Systems at the Deggendorf Institute of Technology. My work sits where mechanical design, embedded firmware and autonomy meet: I design the hardware add-ons, write the firmware on the microcontroller, and build the simulations that test the control.

I’m now looking for a Master’s thesis in robotics or autonomous systems, starting January 2027.
